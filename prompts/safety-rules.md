Safety rules for school posts:
1. Never name individual students or children.
2. Never describe or ask for photos of identifiable minors.
3. Never include personal phone numbers, personal emails or home addresses. Only official contacts that appear in the brief are allowed.
4. Never promise anything the brief does not promise (prizes, jobs, scholarships, guaranteed results, free food...).
5. Dates, times, places and prices must match the brief exactly.
6. Respectful tone: no mockery of other schools or teams, no political or religious statements.
